Initial workshop session

The process starts with a 2-3 hour video call session with your team. During this session, we focus on understanding your business, your target market, and the success criteria for the project. This deep dive allows us to gather essential insights about your business needs and objectives.

Research and Roadmap Development

After the initial session, our team conducts thorough research based on the information collected. We develop a detailed roadmap that outlines the necessary steps to achieve your business goals. This roadmap serves as a strategic guide tailored to your specific requirements.

Delivery and Implementation Options

Once the roadmap is developed, it is provided to you for your use. You can use this roadmap to seek quotes from other agencies or choose to have us implement it. If you opt for our implementation services, the cost of the workshop will be credited against the implementation price, making it a cost-effective option for you.
